Strong brands in 2026 are not chasing novelty for its own sake. They are tightening their visual systems, making websites faster to understand, and designing for trust before flair.

<BlogCallout variant="tip">
Keep your palette tight. Two or three core colours plus neutrals will carry further than a mood board of ten accents.
</BlogCallout>

## Clarity beats complexity

Visitors decide quickly whether a business feels credible. That means readable typography, obvious hierarchy, and contact paths that never feel hidden. The best sites we build feel calm — not empty, but intentional.

## Warmth is the differentiator

Cool, generic layouts are easy to spot. Warm off-whites, thoughtful photography, and human copy help service businesses stand out without looking trendy for a season.

- Lead with one clear promise above the fold
- Use real photography where possible
- Write for humans, not algorithms
